豉椒炒花蛤Clams with Peppers and Fermented Black Beans

做法

  1. 按包装说明处理花蛤。豆豉快冲后沥干略切，灯笼椒切条，姜蒜切末。
  2. 炒锅中火热油，炒香豆豉和姜蒜。下灯笼椒炒1–2分钟，喜欢辣味可加几圈红辣椒。
  3. 下花蛤、料酒、生抽和水，盖盖按包装说明复热至蛤肉达到74°C。开盖翻匀，去掉仍未张口的蛤。
English recipe

Ingredients

  • 400 g Frozen cooked shell-on clams
  • 1 tbsp Fermented black beans
  • 1 Bell pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 10 g Ginger
  • 1 tsp Light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Cooking wine
  • 1 tbsp Neutral oil
  • 60 mL Water

Method

  1. Prepare the clams according to their package. Briefly rinse, drain and chop the black beans. Slice the pepper and mince the ginger and garlic.
  2. Heat oil in a wok over medium heat. Fry the black beans, ginger and garlic until fragrant. Add the bell pepper and fry for 1–2 minutes, adding a few red chili rings if desired.
  3. Add clams, cooking wine, soy sauce and water. Cover and reheat following package directions until the clam meat reaches 74°C. Toss to coat and discard any clams that remain closed.
