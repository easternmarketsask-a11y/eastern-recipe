冬菇滑鸡煲Clay Pot Chicken with Shiitake Mushrooms

分享到社区

约40分钟 · 3人

冬菇提前泡软，鸡腿肉腌好再入煲。

要买什么

做法

  1. 干香菇提前用温水泡软，去蒂切片。鸡腿去骨切成约3厘米的块，用生抽、料酒、糖和淀粉冷藏腌15分钟。
  2. 用可上炉的砂锅或厚底锅，中火用油炒香姜片和葱白。下鸡块炒至表面变色。
  3. 加香菇和水，煮开后盖盖小火焖10–15分钟，直到鸡肉最厚处达到74°C。开盖略收汁，撒葱绿。
English recipe

Ingredients

  • about 500 g, from a whole yellow-feather chicken Chicken thighs
  • 25 g Dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 15 g Ginger
  • 2 Scallions
  • 1 tbsp Light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Cooking wine
  • 2 tsp Potato starch
  • 1/2 tsp Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Neutral oil
  • 120 mL, for braising Water

Method

  1. Soak the mushrooms ahead in warm water until soft, then stem and slice them. Debone the thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces, and marinate with soy sauce, cooking wine, sugar and starch in the refrigerator for 15 minutes.
  2. In a stovetop-safe clay pot or heavy saucepan, fry sliced ginger and scallion whites in oil over medium heat. Add the chicken and stir until opaque on the outside.
  3. Add mushrooms and water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer for 10–15 minutes until the thickest piece reaches 74°C. Uncover to reduce the sauce briefly and scatter with scallion greens.
