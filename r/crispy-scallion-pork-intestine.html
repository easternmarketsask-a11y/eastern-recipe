大葱脆皮肥肠Crispy Pork Intestine with Scallions

分享到社区

约130分钟 · 3–4人

肥肠先卤到软嫩，塞进大葱再煎，外皮酥脆，切开一口葱香。

做法

  1. 冷冻猪大肠先在冰箱冷藏室解冻。生大肠入沸水，待水重新沸腾后煮至少5分钟。倒掉焯水，稍凉后翻开，去掉杂质和多余脂肪，轻轻冲净。处理后洗手，并清洗消毒水槽、台面和用具。
  2. 把清理好的大肠放入干净锅里，加新水没过，放姜片、料酒、生抽和八角。煮沸后转小火炖约75–100分钟，至彻底煮熟、筷子能轻松扎入。捞出沥干，擦干表面。
  3. 大葱切成细条，留少许葱绿装盘。大肠切成约6厘米长的段，每段塞入一束细葱条，两头略露出。
  4. 平底锅加油烧热，用中火分批煎大肠，每面约2–3分钟，翻动至外皮金黄酥脆。出锅斜切成小段，撒上葱绿，趁热吃。
English recipe

Ingredients

  • 600 g Pork large intestine (chitterlings)
  • 20 g Ginger
  • 2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
  • enough to cover, for two batches Water
  • 1 large stalk Large scallion or Chinese leek
  • 2 tbsp Light soy sauce
  • 1 pod Star anise
  • 2 tbsp Neutral cooking oil

Method

  1. Thaw frozen intestines in the refrigerator. Put the raw intestines in boiling water and boil for at least 5 minutes once the water returns to a boil. Discard the water. When cool enough to handle, turn the pieces inside out, trim debris and excess fat, and rinse gently. Wash your hands and clean and sanitize the sink, work surfaces and utensils.
  2. Put the cleaned intestines in a clean pot and cover with fresh water. Add sliced ginger, cooking wine, soy sauce and star anise. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 75–100 minutes until fully cooked and easily pierced with a chopstick. Drain and pat dry.
  3. Cut the scallion into thin batons, reserving some greens for garnish. Cut the intestines into 6 cm lengths and tuck a bundle of scallion batons into each, leaving a little showing at both ends.
  4. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Pan-fry in batches for about 2–3 minutes per side, turning until golden and crisp. Slice diagonally into bite-size pieces, garnish with the scallion greens and serve hot.
