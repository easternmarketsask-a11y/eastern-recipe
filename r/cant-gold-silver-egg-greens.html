金银蛋浸时蔬Choy Sum in Broth with Salted and Century Eggs

分享到社区

约20分钟 · 3人

咸蛋黄炒香，菜心在双蛋汤里煮到刚断生。

做法

  1. 菜心洗净，粗茎对半切。熟咸鸭蛋剥壳，蛋黄压碎、蛋白切丁。皮蛋剥壳切块。
  2. 小火热油，炒香蒜片和咸蛋黄。加入鸡汤和水煮开，放咸蛋白和皮蛋煮约1分钟。
  3. 菜心茎先煮约1分钟，再放叶煮1–2分钟，试味后连汤盛出。
English recipe

Ingredients

  • 350 g Choy sum
  • 1 Cooked salted duck egg
  • 1 Century egg
  • 250 mL Chicken broth
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tsp Neutral oil
  • 100 mL Water

Method

  1. Wash the choy sum and halve thick stems lengthwise. Peel the cooked salted egg, mash the yolk and dice the white. Peel and cut the century egg into chunks.
  2. Heat oil over low heat. Fry sliced garlic and salted egg yolk until fragrant. Bring the broth and water to a boil, then add diced egg white and century egg and simmer for about 1 minute.
  3. Cook the stems for about 1 minute, then add the leaves and cook for another 1–2 minutes. Taste and serve with the broth.
