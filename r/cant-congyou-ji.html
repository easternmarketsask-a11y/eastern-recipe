葱油鸡Scallion Oil Chicken

分享到社区

约35分钟 · 3人

鸡腿浸煮至熟，姜葱用热油激香。

要买什么

  • 带皮鸡腿货架：三黄鸡约500克，取自三黄鸡🧊 冷冻食品

  • 生抽货架：鮮味生抽 500ml1汤匙🧂 干货调料

做法

  1. 一半姜切片，一根葱切段，加水煮开。放鸡腿，补水至刚没过。
  2. 小火微沸约20–25分钟，鸡肉最厚处离骨测温达到74°C。捞出静置5分钟，去骨切片。
  3. 剩余姜葱切细，与盐放进耐热碗。油烧至微微泛波纹，关火后浇在姜葱上。拌入生抽和1汤匙煮鸡的汤，淋在鸡肉上。
English recipe

Ingredients

  • about 500 g, from a whole yellow-feather chicken Skin-on chicken thighs
  • 4 Scallions
  • 20 g Ginger
  • 1 tbsp Light soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp Neutral oil
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • about 1 L Water

Method

  1. Slice half the ginger and cut one scallion into lengths. Bring them to a boil in the water. Add the thighs and top up with just enough water to cover.
  2. Simmer gently for about 20–25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 74°C away from the bone. Rest for 5 minutes, then debone and slice.
  3. Finely chop the remaining ginger and scallions and put them in a heatproof bowl with the salt. Heat the oil until shimmering, turn off the heat and pour it over the aromatics. Add the soy sauce and 1 tbsp poaching liquid, then spoon over the chicken.
